Executive summary
The APAC ex-China region is in a broad manufacturing expansion, with Taiwan PMI at 56.7, Japan at 55.1, South Korea at 53.9, and Singapore at 59.4 all breaching the 52 expansion threshold, while India GDP grew 7.8% and Australia holds rates at 4.35% after three 2026 hikes. The dominant FX event is the July 2026 US-Japan joint yen intervention after a 40-year low, which has introduced significant two-sided JPY risk and uncertainty over the BOJ rate path. Trade policy is a headwind, with the US-China tariff deal on US$30 billion of goods potentially eroding Southeast Asia's tariff edge and slowing China+1 relocation flows. Taiwan's 267.8% YoY surge in outbound investment signals accelerating regional capital deployment that could expand TLD's customer base. Net impact on TLD APAC operations is positive on demand but challenging on cost base and FX translation, with priority actions on JPY hedging, TWD/KRW/SGD hedging, and reassessing Southeast Asia growth assumptions.
🌏 Regional competitiveness outlook (30-90 days)
The regional cost base is under upward pressure from strong currencies in Taiwan, South Korea, and Singapore, which raise EUR-reported costs for TLD entities, while a potentially weaker yen keeps Japanese inputs relatively cheap. Labour and financing costs are rising in Australia and New Zealand, modestly eroding competitiveness in those markets. Overall, the region remains competitive but TLD faces a rising local cost base in its strongest-growth markets over the next 30-90 days.
🏦 Financing / credit outlook (90-180 days)
Regional financing conditions are mixed, with Australia at 4.35% and New Zealand SME spreads at 390bps creating a relatively expensive Oceania funding environment, while Japan remains ultra-loose and Indonesia holds at 4.75%. Strong growth in India, Taiwan, and South Korea supports customer credit quality and lease affordability in those markets. Over the next 90-180 days, the key variable is the BOJ rate path, which could shift JPY funding costs and cross-currency basis materially.
