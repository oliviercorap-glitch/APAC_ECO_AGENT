Watch 🌐 TRADE Taiwan

Proposed US tariffs up to 15% on Taiwanese goods seen as limited hit

Reading
Proposed US tariffs of up to 15 percent on Taiwanese goods are expected to have a limited effect on the machinery sector, according to industry firms cited by Taipei Times. The assessment suggests exporters believe demand inelasticity and product differentiation will absorb much of the duty, though the tariff level itself is material at up to 15 percent on affected categories.
Supply chain impact
Taiwan is a key source of precision components, machine tools, and sub-assemblies that feed GSE manufacturing and aftermarket supply outside Mainland China. A 15 percent duty raises landed cost on any Taiwan-sourced components entering US-bound equipment flows and may push TLD procurement toward dual-sourcing or re-routing via lower-tariff jurisdictions.
Financing / FX impact
Tariff exposure on Taiwan-origin goods affects cost of goods and margin on US-destined sales, with potential TWD volatility around trade-policy headlines. No direct impact on TLD's Taiwan entity financing costs at this stage, but hedging of USD/TWD payables on US-bound component purchases warrants review.
Recommended action
Regional finance team to quantify Taiwan-origin component spend exposed to US-bound flows and model a 15 percent duty scenario within two weeks; procurement to identify alternate sourcing options.
