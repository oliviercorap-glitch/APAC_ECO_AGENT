Executive summary
The APAC region (ex-Mainland China) presents a mixed macroeconomic picture for TLD Group. Thailand's industrial output is contracting sharply, with June factory output falling 3.1% year-on-year, signaling weak demand in a key market. However, China+1 supply chain diversification is accelerating FDI inflows into Vietnam, India, Indonesia, and Thailand, creating new customer bases for GSE in industrial parks and logistics hubs. Most APAC currencies have weakened against the USD, with the Japanese yen, Korean won, and Thai baht under notable pressure, improving export competitiveness for TLD's manufacturing bases but creating FX translation losses when reporting in EUR. Central banks across the region are holding rates steady or cutting cautiously, with the BOJ gradually tightening, keeping financing conditions tight for capital-intensive GSE purchases. Trade agreements like RCEP and CPTPP continue to lower tariffs, benefiting intra-regional component flows. Regulatory environments remain stable with no new capital controls or repatriation restrictions. The net impact on TLD APAC operations is moderately favorable for supply chain diversification and trade costs, but constrained by weak industrial demand in Thailand and Japan and elevated FX hedging costs.

Net competitiveness is stable to slightly improving over the next 30-90 days.
