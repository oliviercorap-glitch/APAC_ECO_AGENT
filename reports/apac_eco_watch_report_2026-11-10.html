Watch 🌐 TRADE Regional

China trade surplus breaches US$1 trillion; tariff escalation risk rises

Reading
China's goods surplus has passed US$1 trillion for the first time, driven by an AI-related surge in chip and computer exports, with October exports up 35.2% year on year and imports also accelerating. Beijing is signalling efforts to address the imbalance, but the scale of the surplus keeps trade tensions with the US and EU elevated ahead of Xi-Trump talks.
Supply chain impact
A record surplus raises the probability of new reciprocal tariffs and export controls, which would spill over onto China+1 sourcing routes through Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia that TLD uses for components and sub-assemblies. Expect added customs complexity and potential rule-of-origin scrutiny on goods transiting these corridors.
Financing / FX impact
Escalation risk supports USD strength versus Asian currencies, a translation headwind for TLD's EUR-reported regional results and a mild tailwind for USD-denominated lease receivables. Volatility in CNY crosses can feed quickly into regional FX hedging costs.
Recommended action
Treasury to stress-test 2027 EUR budget at 3-5% weaker Asian currency crosses versus current spot, and supply chain to map tariff exposure on China+1 component flows within two weeks.
