Reading
China's exports rose 36% year on year in September on strong technology demand and a pre-holiday shipment rush, putting the country on track for another all-time high annual trade surplus. The scale of the surplus implies continued export price competition and sustained pressure on regional currencies and manufacturing margins across Asia. Although Mainland China is out of scope for direct signals, the spillover into APAC ex-China trade, FX, and supply-chain dynamics is material.
Supply chain impact
A 36% Chinese export surge intensifies competitive pressure on APAC ex-China manufacturers, including TLD's regional suppliers and contract manufacturers, potentially compressing their margins and forcing price renegotiation.
