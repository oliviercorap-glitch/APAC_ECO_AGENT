Executive summary
The APAC region ex-Mainland China presents a broadly favorable macroeconomic environment for TLD Group's regional operations. Manufacturing PMIs are stabilizing with India expanding strongly at 55.4 and South Korea showing sustained growth, while Vietnam's FDI inflows reached US$17.30 billion in August, confirming its status as a leading China+1 destination. Regional central banks are in easing or pause mode, lowering financing costs across TLD's footprint and creating potential demand tailwinds for customer-financed equipment. Trade integration is deepening through RCEP and CPTPP expansion, easing cross-border operations. Key watch items include rising transfer pricing scrutiny in Japan, Korea, and Australia, and refined petroleum product price pressures on logistics costs.
