Executive summary
The APAC ex-China picture this cycle is dominated by trade policy uncertainty and selective FDI strength. India stands out with Q1 FY27 FDI inflows of INR 1,13,283 crore, up 17% YoY, breaching the 15% threshold and confirming its China+1 momentum, while Vietnam continues to attract roughly US$18.5 billion in net FDI despite unresolved US reciprocal tariff exposure. The upcoming Xi-Trump summit and a potential US$60 billion US-China tariff-reduction deal are the swing factor for regional supply chain relocation and Asian FX. New Zealand's RBNZ surprised markets by holding the OCR at 2.75% versus 3.00% consensus, a mildly dovish signal for the NZD. Net impact on TLD APAC is modestly positive on industrial demand but carries FX translation and tariff risk; priority actions are India exposure review and Vietnam FX stress-testing.
🌏 Regional competitiveness outlook (30-90 days)
Regional cost bases remain stable with India strengthening as a manufacturing and customer market and Vietnam retaining its China+1 position despite tariff overhang. A US-China tariff truce would temper relocation-driven demand growth in Southeast Asia and India, while a breakdown would accelerate it.
🏦 Financing / credit outlook (90-180 days)
Regional financing conditions are broadly accommodative, with New Zealand pausing at 2.75% and India's strong inflows supporting lower country risk premia. Watch for renewed RBNZ tightening and any tariff-driven FX volatility that could raise hedging costs over the next 90-180 days.
