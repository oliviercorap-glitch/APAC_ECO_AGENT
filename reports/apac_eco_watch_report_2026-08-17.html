Executive summary
The APAC region (ex-Mainland China) presents a mixed but generally stable macroeconomic picture for TLD Group, with India and Vietnam leading industrial expansion (PMI above 52) while Japan remains in contraction near 49. Thailand's Q2 GDP beat and Taiwan's upgraded 11.05% growth forecast signal resilience, supported by favorable FDI inflows from China+1 diversification and deepening trade agreements under RCEP and CPTPP. Currency markets are stable with no moves exceeding the 3% threshold, though the yen remains weak near 150/USD. Monetary policy is broadly accommodative outside Japan, with BoJ gradually hiking to 0.5% while RBA, BOK, BSP, BOT, and BI hold or ease. The critical concern is the deteriorating risk environment over the next 6 months, driven by potential US tariff actions on APAC exports, China slowdown spillover, and geopolitical tensions in the Taiwan Strait and South China Sea. Net impact on TLD APAC operations is moderately positive for demand and sourcing, but requires proactive risk management on tariffs and currency volatility.
