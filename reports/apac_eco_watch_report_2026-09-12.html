Critical 💱 FX Japan

ALERT — Yen carry trade unwinding as JPY strengthens, funding stress risk

Reading
The yen is strengthening and analysts are questioning whether the long-standing carry trade — borrowing cheap yen to fund higher-yielding overseas assets — is beginning to unravel. An unwind forces leveraged positions to be closed, which historically produces sharp, correlated moves across regional currencies and credit spreads rather than a gradual drift.
Supply chain impact
A disorderly unwind would raise hedging and working-capital costs for TLD's Japanese entity and for yen-denominated sourcing or service contracts, and could disrupt Japanese supplier payment terms and component lead times across the APAC network.
Financing / FX impact
TLD's Japan entity results translate into EUR at a stronger yen, a favourable translation effect, but any yen-denominated debt or intercompany balances become more expensive to service in local terms if funding costs spike. Regional risk assets and high-yield credit would reprice wider, raising customer financing costs for GSE leases in Australia, Singapore and Korea.
Recommended action
CFO APAC should immediately quantify TLD's net yen exposure and any carry-linked counterparty exposure, and stress-test the Japan entity and regional lease book against a further 3-5% yen appreciation within 48 hours.
