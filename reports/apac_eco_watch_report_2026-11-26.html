Executive summary
The APAC ex-China picture is bifurcated: strong manufacturing momentum in Vietnam (PMI 52.3) and the Philippines (fastest production growth since 2016) and a Thai FDI surge of 68.3% YoY sit alongside a 7.7% YTD baht depreciation, a 17.8% YoY Philippine FDI decline, and a hawkish turn from the BOK (3.25%) and RBA (held at 3.60% but discussing hikes). US tariff threats cloud the 2027 ASEAN export outlook with Vietnam most exposed, while India's FDI and liberalization momentum and CPTPP reaffirmation offer offsets. Net impact on TLD APAC is mixed: FX translation losses in Thailand and tighter Philippine funding are the immediate negatives, while Vietnam and Thailand industrial expansion are demand positives. Priority actions are the 48-hour THB hedge and translation review, the Philippine FDI/funding stress-test, and the Vietnam tariff-exposure stress-test.
🌏 Regional competitiveness outlook (30-90 days)
Baht weakness (7.7% YTD, projected to 34.50 by Q3 2027) and continued Vietnam/Philippines manufacturing expansion improve regional export competitiveness and industrial demand over the next 30-90 days. Rising Korean and Australian rate expectations raise the regional cost of capital, modestly offsetting the demand tailwind.
🏦 Financing / credit outlook (90-180 days)
Regional financing conditions are tightening at the margin: BOK at 3.25%, RBA hawkish at 3.60%, and Thai bond yields rising, while Philippine FDI weakness signals reduced intercompany borrowing capacity. Expect higher local funding costs and wider risk premia across APAC over 90-180 days, with India and Thailand the relative bright spots on inflows.
