Executive summary
The APAC region ex-Mainland China presents a broadly expansionary but increasingly divergent economic picture in August 2026. Japan and the Philippines lead with PMI readings of 54.9, while Indonesia has slipped back into contraction at 49.8. The Bank of Korea's second consecutive 25bps hike to 3.00% marks a clear monetary tightening trend in Northeast Asia, contrasting with Thailand's hold stance. The coordinated US-Japan FX intervention to support the yen represents a critical policy shift with direct implications for TLD's JPY exposure. India's manufacturing sector, while cooling to a five-year low PMI of 53.5, continues to attract strong FDI inflows up 18% YoY. Trade liberalization momentum is building with Thailand's first EU FTA and CPTPP accession talks for the Philippines and Indonesia. Net impact on TLD APAC operations is mixed: rising financing costs in Korea, currency stabilization in Japan, and continued expansion opportunities in the Philippines and Vietnam.
