Executive summary
The APAC ex-China picture is bifurcated: structural fundamentals are favorable, with China+1 FDI flowing into Vietnam, Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, India, and the Philippines, RCEP and CPTPP deepening trade integration, and most central banks easing or holding. Against this, the six-month risk outlook is rated unfavorable and deteriorating on renewed US–China trade escalation, potential US tariff spillover onto ASEAN and India, USD strength-driven EM currency stress, and China slowdown transmission. Manufacturing PMIs are mixed, with India and Indonesia above 50, Vietnam and Thailand near the line, and Japan and Korea subdued, but no country has breached the 48 or 52 threshold this month. FX is stable overall, with JPY, KRW, THB, PHP, and IDR under periodic pressure but no 3% 30-day breach, and no single rate decision exceeds 50bps. Net impact on TLD APAC is modestly positive on demand and financing costs but carries elevated tail risk from trade and FX shocks. Priority actions are the 48-hour regional risk review, a China+1 footprint review, and refreshed FX hedging and customer credit scoring.
🌏 Regional competitiveness outlook (30-90 days)
Regional cost competitiveness is broadly stable to improving, supported by China+1 FDI inflows, RCEP rules-of-origin savings, and rate cuts in Indonesia, Thailand, and the Philippines. Japan and Korea remain the weak spots on subdued external demand and BOJ normalization, while Vietnam and Thailand sit near the 50 PMI line with electronics and auto-linked softness. Over the next 30–90 days, expect continued divergence rather than a uniform regional shift.
🏦 Financing / credit outlook (90-180 days)
Regional financing conditions are easing in Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, and cautiously in India, lowering funding costs for TLD entities and GSE lease customers in those markets. Japan is the outlier, with BOJ normalization gradually raising JPY funding costs. USD firmness and EM currency stress are the key risks that could reverse this benign trend over the next 90–180 days.
