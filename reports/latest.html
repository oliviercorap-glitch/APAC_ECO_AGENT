Executive summary
The APAC ex-Mainland China picture is mixed, with manufacturing momentum diverging across the region. Japan PMI eased to a seven-month low of 53.3 (still expansion), Vietnam rebounded to 52.3 above the 52 threshold, Indonesia improved with six-month-high optimism, while the Philippines PMI fell below 50 for only the third time, the key alert. Net impact on TLD APAC is modestly negative on Philippine credit risk and INR/JPY translation, offset by Thai easing and Vietnamese/Indonesian expansion; priority actions are the Philippine credit review and FX threshold monitoring.
