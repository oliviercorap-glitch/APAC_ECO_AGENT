Executive summary
APAC ex-China presents a divergent picture: India and Vietnam remain firmly expansionary (Vietnam PMI 53.0, FDI up 32% YoY to USD 18.24bn), the Philippines is accelerating (PMI 54.9), while Japan (PMI 53.3, a 7-month low), South Korea and Thailand sit in mild contraction. The dominant FX event is the unprecedented US-Japan joint yen intervention after a 40-year low, which caps yen depreciation but introduces two-way volatility and raises the EUR cost base of Japanese operations. Regional monetary policy has largely peaked with BOK, BI, BOT and BSP easing, lowering customer financing costs, while the BOJ normalizes as the outlier. China+1 FDI continues to favor Vietnam, India and Indonesia, expanding TLD's sourcing and customer options. Priority actions are immediate JPY hedge review, tighter local-currency hedge discipline, and rebalancing customer-financing appetite toward expanding markets.
🌏 Regional competitiveness outlook (30-90 days)
Regional cost base is broadly stable to slightly favorable, with easing monetary policy and China+1 FDI supporting industrial activity, but yen appreciation and USD strength add cost and translation pressure in Japan and import-dependent markets over the next 30-90 days.
🏦 Financing / credit outlook (90-180 days)
Regional financing conditions are improving as BOK, BI, BOT and BSP ease, lowering customer and entity funding costs, though BOJ normalization and JGB volatility plus a stronger-for-longer USD keep hedging and cross-border funding costs elevated over 90-180 days.
