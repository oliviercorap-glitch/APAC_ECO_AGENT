Executive summary
The APAC ex-China region presents a mixed picture: India shows strong GDP growth of 7.8% with investment up 11.9% but manufacturing PMI decelerating to a five-year low of 52.8, while South Korea has begun a hiking cycle for the first time since 2023 and Australia holds rates with inflation above 3% through 2026. Malaysia's PMI at 50.1 signals flat industrial momentum, and South Asian sovereign debt dispersion (40% to 131% of GDP) informs country risk premia. Net impact on TLD APAC is moderately negative on financing costs from Korean and Australian tightening, offset by Indian investment-driven demand potential. Priority actions are repricing Korean and Australian lease assumptions, monitoring India PMI for a breach below 52, and updating the South Asia country risk matrix.
🌏 Regional competitiveness outlook (30-90 days)
Regional cost bases are under mild upward pressure from Korean and Australian monetary tightening and persistent Australian inflation, while India's investment boom and Malaysia's stability offer offsetting opportunities. India's manufacturing slowdown bears watching as a potential drag on industrial capex appetite over the next 30-90 days.
🏦 Financing / credit outlook (90-180 days)
Financing conditions are tightening at the margin in South Korea and Australia, with higher customer lease costs likely over the next 90-180 days, while India's strong growth supports stable credit conditions there. Malaysia and most of South Asia remain stable, but sovereign debt dispersion across South Asia warrants differentiated country risk premia in treasury funding decisions.
