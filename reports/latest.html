Executive summary
The APAC ex-China region shows broad manufacturing expansion with two threshold breaches: Indonesia's PMI rebounded to 52.4 from 49.8 and Thailand's rose to 54.3, both crossing the 52 expansion mark, while Japan's FDI surged 142.7% YoY to USD 45.81 billion, far exceeding the 15% swing threshold. India's GDP accelerated to 7.8% with investments up 11.9%, reinforcing its China+1 role, while the Philippines remains weak at 50.1 and Taiwan's NT dollar faces substantial depreciation pressure. Net impact on TLD APAC operations is positive on demand and supplier activity, with FX translation headwinds concentrated in Taiwan and rising financing costs in Japan as BOJ normalisation accelerates. Priority actions are to confirm the Indonesia and Thailand PMI breaches, assess Japan funding capacity against the FDI surge, and verify the TWD move against the 3% threshold.
