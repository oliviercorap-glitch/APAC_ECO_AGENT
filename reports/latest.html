Executive summary
The APAC ex-China region presents a mixed but broadly constructive picture, with Taiwan's Manufacturing PMI at a strong 60.7 and Indonesia at 51.43 confirming industrial expansion, while Malaysia sits marginally above neutral at 50.1. Monetary policy is the dominant risk vector: Australia's RBA has hiked five times in 2026 to 4.85%, the highest among major G20 economies, and South Korea has resumed tightening with cumulative 25bps moves, raising financing costs for TLD entities and customers in both markets. FX pressures are building, with Thailand intervening against baht appreciation and the Philippines facing a widening net liability position, while Hong Kong's peg debate adds structural uncertainty to treasury assumptions. Net impact on TLD APAC operations is moderately negative on financing costs and customer capex appetite in Australia and Korea, offset by healthy industrial demand in Taiwan and Indonesia. Priority actions are repricing Australian and Korean customer financing, reviewing AUD and THB hedging, and confirming Taiwan supplier lead times given the PMI threshold breach.
