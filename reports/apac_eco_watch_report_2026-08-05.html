Executive summary
The APAC region outside Mainland China is experiencing significant monetary policy divergence, with South Korea and Indonesia implementing rate hikes while India holds steady and Australia faces further tightening. Indonesia has been the most aggressive, with a cumulative 75bps of hikes in under two months including an emergency move, while the rupiah has depreciated 2.2% in a single month. India's manufacturing PMI has eased to 53.5, its weakest since August 2021, and net FDI turned negative in May, signaling deteriorating external financing conditions. Vietnam's manufacturing PMI rose to 52.9, confirming its status as a leading China+1 beneficiary with strong expansion momentum. Japan's record ¥11.7 trillion FX intervention, now with US participation, highlights ongoing yen weakness and currency instability. The regional tightening bias across India, Indonesia, Philippines, South Korea, and Vietnam signals rising financing costs for TLD entities and customers across multiple markets. Priority actions include reviewing Indonesian and Korean financing arrangements, assessing INR exposure given negative FDI, and updating regional financial forecasts to reflect the tightening cycle.
🌏 Regional competitiveness outlook (30-90 days)
Vietnam's manufacturing strength and FDI inflows position it as the region's most competitive manufacturing destination, while India's slowing PMI and negative FDI suggest moderating competitiveness. Indonesia's currency instability and emergency rate hikes raise its operational cost base, and Japan's yen weakness makes its manufacturing exports more competitive but creates translation losses for TLD.
🏦 Financing / credit outlook (90-180 days)
Regional financing conditions are tightening across multiple markets, with South Korea expected to reach 3.25% by Q1 2027 and Indonesia at 5.50% after emergency hikes. Australia faces a potential fourth rate hike despite slowing growth, while India's rate pause may be temporary. TLD should expect higher financing costs across most APAC markets over the next 90-180 days.
