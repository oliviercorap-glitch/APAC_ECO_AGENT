Taiwan's industrial production rose over 23% year-on-year to a record high and the ADB raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast to 11%, both driven by AI-related manufacturing demand. Meanwhile, the US-China tariff truce extension provides welcome near-term trade stability, though the underlying truce remains temporary. The US Treasury yield surge poses the most immediate risk to TLD's regional financing costs and FX exposure, particularly for USD-linked customer lease financing. Net impact on TLD APAC operations is moderately positive on demand fundamentals but negative on financing costs and input pricing from Taiwan.
